Account Deletion Request - HomeSarthi

HomeSarthi allows users to permanently delete their account directly from within the mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the HomeSarthi mobile application.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Go to the Profile or Settings section.
  4. Tap on "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your request.

Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Profile information
  • Property listings (if you are an owner)
  • Uploaded images
  • Chat messages
  • Associated account activity data

Data Retention

Account deletion is processed immediately or within 7 working days. We may retain certain information if required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.
